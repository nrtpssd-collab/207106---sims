Field lines

Rules to check: lines start on + and end on −. The number of lines is proportional to |q|. Lines never cross. Where they crowd together, E is strong.
Misconception test: drop a test charge from rest near the dipole. Does it travel along a curved field line?

Notice: far away the dipole field falls as 1/r³ (slope −3), faster than a single charge's 1/r² (slope −2). On the axis E is twice as large as on the equator, and on the equator E points opposite to p.
